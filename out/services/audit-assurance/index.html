Service Details

Audit & Assurance

Independent audit and assurance solutions that strengthen confidence and governance.

Service Overview

Our Audit & Assurance practice delivers objective financial and operational assurance with a focus on risk, controls and reporting integrity. We combine regulatory rigor with business understanding to support stakeholder confidence.

Key Offerings

Statutory Audit

Independent statutory audits aligned with applicable standards and regulations.

Tax Audit

Tax audits focused on compliance quality and reporting accuracy.

Internal Audit

Control-oriented internal audit programs to improve governance and efficiency.

Concurrent Audit

Continuous transaction review to support control discipline in real time.

Bank Branch Audit

Specialized branch-level audits for banking operations and reporting.

Stock Audit

Inventory verification and valuation review to strengthen reporting reliability.

Revenue Audit

Revenue process audits to identify leakages and improve billing assurance.

IS Audit

Technology control audits evaluating IT systems, access and reliability.

Due Diligence Reviews

Financial and control due diligence for strategic decisions and transactions.

Risk-Based Audits

Audit planning and execution prioritizing high-impact business risks.

Management Audits

Process and managerial audits to improve decision controls and performance.

Special Purpose Audits

Targeted audit assignments for specific compliance and assurance objectives.

Benefits

Improved governance and control maturity

High confidence in financial reporting

Proactive risk identification and mitigation

Stronger readiness for regulators and stakeholders
